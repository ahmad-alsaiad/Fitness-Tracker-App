1) Daily Tracking & Biometrics
    a) Morning body weight, subjective notes (sleep, stress, energy).
    b) Dual-Strategy Weight Tracking: Daily continuous logging for trend lines + a fixed weekly weigh-in day for your coach check-in.
    c) Physique Photo Vault: Uploading front, back, and side photos organized by date.
    d) Photo Comparison Tool: Side-by-side viewer / slider to visually compare physique photos between any two dates.

2) Nutrition & Macro Target Management
    a) Manual entry of final daily calories and macros (calculated externally, e.g., in MyFitnessPal).
    b) Visual indicator showing compliance against your coach's daily targets.

3) Schedule-Driven Navigation & Coach Programming
    a) Calendar and weekly nav bar interface.
    b) Prescribed resistance training exercises, sets, reps, and cardio sessions.
    c) Progressive Overload View: Seeing last week's numbers while logging today's lifts.
    d) Automatic PR Tracker: Highlights personal records when you beat previous strength milestones.

4) Accountability & Exception Handling
    a) Retroactive Editing: Full, permanent ability to edit or backfill any past day's data without breaking trends.
    b) Workout Cancellation & Reason Tagging: Option to cancel a scheduled workout with a specific reason.
    c) Justified vs. Unjustified Tags: Automatic color-coding (Green for things outside your control like sickness/travel, Red for internal slips like procrastination/low motivation).

5) Daily Habits & Non-Negotiables Checklist
    a) A side daily task list for habits you must abide by every day (e.g., drinking 2.5L of water, step goals, supplements, sleep targets).

6) The "Coach Check-In" Hub
    a) Weekly Summary View: Aggregates your past 7 days (fixed weekly weight, average calories, workout completion, habits compliance).
    b) Running Scratchpad / Notes: A place to drop random thoughts, questions, or notes throughout the week so you never forget them on check-in day.
